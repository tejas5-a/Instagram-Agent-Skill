# Command: `/ig-human`

## Overview
Rewrites stiff, corporate, robotic, or AI-sounding copy into natural, authentic, conversational creator language while preserving the original core message.

## Trigger & Syntax
```bash
/ig-human [text="..."] [tone="conversational|punchy|direct|witty"]
```

## Banned AI Slop Clichés
This command actively identifies and purges dead giveaways of LLM-generated social copy:
- ❌ *"In today's fast-paced digital world / landscape / ecosystem"*
- ❌ *"Unlock your potential"*
- ❌ *"A game-changer for..."*
- ❌ *"Delve into / Dive deep into"*
- ❌ *"Look no further"*
- ❌ *"Without further ado"*
- ❌ *"In conclusion / To sum it up"*
- ❌ Excessive rhetorical questions and hollow buzzwords

## Output Format
1. **Humanized Copy**: Clean, natural creator tone with natural cadence and scannable formatting.
2. **Excised Clichés**: List of specific generic words removed.
3. **Pacing Notes**: Why the new rhythm works better on Instagram.
