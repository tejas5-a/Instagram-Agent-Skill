# Command: `/ig-plan`

## Overview
Builds sustainable weekly or monthly content publishing calendars grounded in 4 proven content pillars and optimal algorithmic windows.

## Trigger & Syntax
```bash
/ig-plan [niche="..."] [duration=7days|30days] [frequency=3x|5x|daily] [pillars="..."]
```

## The 4 Content Pillars
1. **Educational (40%)**: Frameworks, how-tos, tutorials, cheat sheets (drives saves).
2. **Entertaining / Relatable (20%)**: Memes, industry truths, shared experiences (drives shares).
3. **Inspiring / Proof (30%)**: Case studies, founder stories, transformations (builds authority).
4. **Converting (10%)**: Direct offers, lead magnets, service announcements (generates revenue).

## Output Format
1. **Pillar Allocation Breakdown**
2. **Day-by-Day Publishing Schedule**:
   - Day of week
   - Recommended content format (Reel, Carousel, Story Arc, Single Post)
   - Specific topic angle & hook
   - Assigned content pillar
   - Optimal posting window (e.g., `11:30 AM` or `6:30 PM`)
3. **Target Performance Indicators (KPIs)**: Save benchmarks, completion rates, and follower acquisition targets.
