# Command: `/ig-growth`

## Overview
Develops realistic, sustainable organic growth strategies based on content positioning, discoverability, retention mechanics, and community engagement.

> 🚫 **Zero-Tolerance Rule**: Never promise guaranteed follower numbers, explosive overnight spikes, or shady engagement groups.

## Trigger & Syntax
```bash
/ig-growth [niche="..."] [currentFollowers=...] [goal="..."] [timeframe="..."]
```

## The Sustainable Growth Engine
- **Top of Funnel (Discoverability)**: High-hook Reels + SEO Name Field + Relevant Niche Hashtags.
- **Middle of Funnel (Authority & Retention)**: In-depth Carousels + Actionable Cheat Sheets.
- **Bottom of Funnel (Community & Conversion)**: Daily interactive Stories + DM conversations + Email lead magnets.

## Output Format
1. **Audience Research & Positioning Angle**
2. **Discoverability Strategy (Search & Explore Optimization)**
3. **Daily / Weekly Consistency Blueprint**
4. **Collaboration & Community Engagement Strategy**
5. **Monthly Measurable Growth Milestones**
