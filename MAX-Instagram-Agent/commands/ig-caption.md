# Command: `/ig-caption`

## Overview
Generates compelling Instagram captions designed for scannability, reader retention, and conversation triggering.

## Trigger & Syntax
```bash
/ig-caption [topic="..."] [niche="..."] [tone="conversational|direct|story|witty"] [cta="comment|save|dm|link"]
```

## Caption Anatomy
- **Line 1 (Above the Fold)**: 125 characters or fewer before Instagram cuts off with `...more`. Must provoke curiosity or deliver an immediate payoff.
- **Body**: Short, punchy paragraphs with line breaks. No wall of text.
- **Call-to-Action**: Specific prompt that gives readers a low-friction reason to interact.
- **Hashtags**: Categorized and spaced neatly at the end.

## Output Format
1. **Primary Caption**: Complete copy with strong opening hook and clear CTA.
2. **Alternative Caption**: Shorter, punchier storytelling variation.
3. **Dedicated Hook Alternatives**: 3 one-liner opening hooks.
4. **Targeted CTA**: Explicit conversation or bookmark prompt.
5. **Niche-Specific Hashtags**: Separated into Niche (5), Community (4), and Broad (3).
