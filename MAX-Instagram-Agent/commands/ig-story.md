# Command: `/ig-story`

## Overview
Architects high-engagement 9:16 Instagram Story sequences that utilize interactive sticker widgets (Polls, Quizzes, Sliders, Question boxes) to drive direct messages and community relationship depth.

## Trigger & Syntax
```bash
/ig-story [topic="..."] [objective="engagement|dms|leads|feedback"] [slides=3-6]
```

## Story Sequence Arc
1. **Slide 1 (Context & Hook)**: Relatable moment or surprising question.
2. **Slide 2 (Micro-Story / Value)**: Quick behind-the-scenes or lesson.
3. **Slide 3 (Interactive Sticker)**: Poll (Yes/No), Quiz, or Emoji Slider to lower friction.
4. **Slide 4 (Open Question / DM Invitation)**: Prompting replies to your inbox.
5. **Final Slide (CTA / Resource Link)**: Link sticker or keyword trigger.

## Output Format
1. **Story Arc Theme & Objective**
2. **Slide-by-Slide Directions**:
   - Slide Number
   - Exact text overlay
   - Interactive sticker type & wording (Poll, Quiz, Slider, Question Box)
   - Visual staging & photo/video background suggestion
3. **DM Conversation Trigger & Final CTA**
